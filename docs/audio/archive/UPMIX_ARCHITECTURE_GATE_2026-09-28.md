## Question and preselected comparison

The owner chose to continue the offline investigation before the listening session. Existing tests had two distinct failures: learned separators kept too little eligible instrument energy while leaking protected sources, and the original C renderer still swung on **8/40** songs when given true eligible audio. A global membership latch, per-source activity latch, simple renderer knobs, and static-knob CMA had already failed their respective screens. The specific question here was whether the **ideal bounded real mask reconstructed as eligible audio** could pass the full-40 renderer gates using front retention **0.70**, a value already selected on the earlier true-audio DEV follow-up. No new knob value was chosen from this result.

## What drives the original true-audio swings?

The earlier [swing-timing diagnostic](UPMIX_ORACLE_RENDERER_PROBE_2026-09-28.md) located 10 swinging stem traces across eight original true-audio swing songs. Six of the eight songs had no qualifying vocal entry in the excerpt. In eight of ten traces, the higher rear share occurs while the instrument itself is quieter; in four traces at least one extreme is below −45 dBFS. The frozen numerical swing gate still counts these events, but their audibility is unverified.

[`diag_oracle_depth_components.py`](../../../tools/stereo-upmix-render/diag_oracle_depth_components.py) now remeasures those **same one-second extreme windows**. A cell belongs to a true stem when its energy is at least 10 dB above the other stems and above 300 Hz; a 53-ms frame requires more than 20 owned cells. For each window, the script reproduces the metric's median of framewise rear shares, then reports median front and rear output energy **per unit of that stem's energy** in the owned cells. The reproduced ten share differences differ from the saved timing report by at most **0.001**. This matters: summing energies over a whole second instead of reproducing the framewise median gave a wrong-sign answer on a sparse piano passage and was discarded.

| Original true-audio baseline | **10/10** | **+6.14 dB** | **−2.20 dB** | Rear in **10/10** |

These are **fixed-window mechanism comparisons**, not new full-song swing counts. The full oracle screen separately found 8→5/20 songs swinging with air off and 8→1/20 with retention .75; the full-40 checks rejected both on level or string share. The fixed-window comparison shows that air removal reduces part of the rear-side variation but leaves most of it, while front retention mostly hides the rear variation in the share ratio by changing the front. It is consistent with the measured instrument-share loss. The rear component includes object, ambience and air, and the owned-cell filter cannot perfectly isolate one source, so this is output-energy attribution, **not** an identified internal C gain coefficient or proof of what the owner hears. Detailed per-trace values are in `D:\upmix\runs\oracle_renderer_probe\depth_components{,_rear_air_off,_front_retention075}.json`; [`run_oracle_depth_components.ps1`](../../../tools/stereo-upmix-render/pc/run_oracle_depth_components.ps1) reproduces them without a new render.

## Architecture decision

**Yes: the current solution has two measured architectural bottlenecks.** The present learned extractor/mixture-routing chain cannot simultaneously deliver confident instruments and reject protected stems at stable gain; more epochs and a generic activity latch have not moved that frontier. The ideal bounded real-mask **audio** proves that a real time-frequency mask output is not inherently incapable, but the trained models remain far from it and are not causal as written. Separately, the real C renderer moves the measured rear depth with **true** eligible audio, so training the extractor alone cannot guarantee the stability target. Front retention can suppress the swing score while costing strings or keys; it is not a complete remedy.

The next offline change should target **the rear object/bed path itself at retained instrument level**, with true eligible audio as the first controlled input and ideal-mask audio second. A host-only prototype can hold a source-aware rear transfer ratio across instrument continuity while keeping fast protection and the front residual explicit; the fixed-window diagnostic should show the rear-side +6 dB variation falling without making the rear quieter or reducing string/key share. Then rerun the real-C full40 gate, an independent evaluation set that has not already steered the design, and source-attributed entry/exit metrics. Only after that bound works should another causal eligible-audio model be trained, selected by the C renderer and transition metrics rather than SI-SDR alone. Track A owns any eventual hook change; device int8 load, ≤85-ms latency and owner listening remain separate required gates.

**Subsequent result:** the [host-only direct-object probe](UPMIX_DIRECT_OBJECT_RENDERER_PROBE_2026-09-28.md) completed this first renderer-bound test. Its frozen direct/no-bed/body-0 route passed the measured DEV40 render gates with both true eligible audio and ideal bounded-mask audio, but the existing sep9 estimate still missed guitar/keys/piano and bass on the enriched-20 screen. The current next step is a causal estimator and a new adequately covered independent set, not more tuning of the failed front-retention point.

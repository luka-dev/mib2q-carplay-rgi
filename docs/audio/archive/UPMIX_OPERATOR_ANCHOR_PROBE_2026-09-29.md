# Causal Slow-Operator Anchor Probe

_2026-09-29. Review 42. Architecture, training comparison and gates frozen before calibration or DEV measurement._

Reviews [39](UPMIX_SPATIAL_OVERLAP_BOUND_2026-09-29.md)–[41](UPMIX_CUE_ATTRIBUTION_BOUND_2026-09-29.md) isolate the gap: a true-stem-informed full stereo operator passes the inspected-DEV median source/entry screen, but fitting it from the current causal student leaks vocal and drums. Privileged removal of those protected contributions repairs selectivity while reducing eligible transmission. Test one learned alternative: keep an explicit **slow spatial operator** and anchor it during training to a true-stem fit from a disjoint earlier window, while the ordinary eligible-audio and source-attribution losses keep the output audible and protected. Stems supply training targets only; inference receives stereo alone.

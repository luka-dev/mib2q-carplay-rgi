## Hypothesis and frozen setup

The [source-loss pair](UPMIX_CAUSAL_SOURCE_PAIR_2026-09-28.md) opened a single eligible mask and increased vocal/bass transmission with eligible retention. The next test makes the eligible mask one output of four competing per-bin source probabilities (vocal, drums/percussion, bass, eligible). The head has the same number of scalar outputs as the existing stereo complex-mask head but shares the selected eligible probability across L/R. The backbone remains causal in time, with 2-second streaming level normalisation and zero lookahead. The role head is **586,916 parameters and 125 M estimated neural MAC/s** at 48 kHz/512-hop, before STFT, renderer and device int8 measurement. Whole-file Python inference does not certify streaming behavior or latency.

The four-role target uses aligned stem power in each time/frequency cell. Cross-entropy trains all four heads; quarter-power cell weights limit domination by high-energy bass while downweighting silence. The original audio reconstruction bundle remains active. Four fixed CPU batches gave baseline/role gradient ratios **.657/.775/3.049/1.086**. `--role-ce .279254` fixes the role gradient to 30% of the median baseline parameter-gradient norm at initialization. The weight was set before seeing DEV performance. The prior 4k causal shared-mask anchor from the source-loss pair is the control; the new role model uses the same train/pseudo pools, half/half sampling, seed, 8-second crops, batch 12, 4k steps, learning rate 3e-4 and warm-up 2k. The output head and auxiliary loss are the only model/objective changes.

## Selection rule fixed before training

First, use the same 12 DEV excerpts as `diag_gap.py` and separately attribute the estimated mask's **reanalyzed** transmission to eligible, vocal, drums/percussion and bass. Compare median *per-song* protected/eligible transmission ratios with the frozen 4k anchor; those ratios are invariant to a global output gain. A positive selectivity signal requires eligible recall ≥.43 (anchor .46 minus .03) and vocal, drum and bass ratios each ≤80% of anchor. The 20-song transition diagnostic must have vocal-entry retention median ≥.85 and p10 ≥.60. These are gates for considering a longer train, not product acceptance.

Regardless of diagnostic direction, the fixed host C renderer will screen the same enriched DEV20. First render with student injection gain 1.0. Set one follow-up candidate gain to `10^(−paired-median-rear-RMS-difference/20)` and rerender the same songs; call the levels matched only if the actual paired-median rear RMS is within ±.1 dB. The gain is applied to the injected student audio before both C conditions, with byte-identical injection checked within each pair. Compare instrument and protected-source shares at that matched level before claiming a useful gain. The product thresholds remain guitar ≥.15, keys ≥.15, piano ≥.10, strings ≥.14, voice ≤.02, bass ≤.06, drums ≤.08, ≤5/39 any-stem swing songs on full40, level slopes within ±.05, vocal-entry median/p10 ≥.85/.60. A positive 4k result requires a matched-age longer run and full40 plus truly independent stems. Device int8 ≤150 M MAC/s, ≤85 ms extra latency, Track A port and the owner's blind live CarPlay verdict remain separate gates.

The 4k run completed cleanly in 884 seconds. Step-4000 DEV SI-SDR was **−1.35 dB**, compared with the frozen anchor's **−1.49 dB**; its training evaluator's vocal-leak score was **.087** versus **.110**. The fixed 12-song source-attribution diagnostic shows why that apparent leak improvement is insufficient:

| Drums/percussion | .0270 | .0280 |
| Bass | .0154 | .0150 |
| Vocal / eligible, per-song median | .4050 | .4352 |

All four preselected selectivity gates failed: eligible recall fell by .112 instead of remaining within .03, and none of the protected/eligible ratios fell to ≤80% of the anchor. Lower absolute vocal transmission mainly comes from a more closed mask. On the 20-song transition diagnostic (43 vocal entries), entry post/pre eligible retention median/p10 worsened **.835/.588→.788/.539**, missing both product thresholds. The role model has no selection signal for a 10k continuation. Its step-4000 checkpoint SHA-256 is `7016E10289BCE7F6059A48985BB80911449B66C3E4C37B26FDFC5221C4D2E3CD`.

The precommitted real-C enriched DEV20 screen at injection gain 1.0 completed with 115 stem rows and byte-identical injected WAVs between its original/direct C conditions. The selected direct renderer changed any-stem swings **3→0/20** (none new). Compared directly with the frozen anchor's direct-C output, guitar fell **.104→.087**, keys stayed **.143→.143**, piano fell **.109→.105**, vocals rose **.0157→.0182** and bass rose **.0680→.0722**. The candidate's paired-median rear RMS was **−.4827 dB** relative to the anchor. The single gain specified by the frozen formula was therefore **1.05714607093032**.

The matched-gain C rerender reached **+.0001 dB paired-median rear RMS**, well within the frozen ±.1 dB tolerance. Both the anchor and matched candidate had **0/20** any-stem swing songs. At the same rear level, candidate guitar/keys/piano/strings were **.0996/.1648/.1100/.2065**; voice/bass/drums were **.0207/.0834/.0127**. Guitar misses ≥.15, vocals miss ≤.02, and bass misses ≤.06. Keys, piano and strings pass on this enriched DEV20, but cannot rescue the failures or establish full40/independent performance. This is a real host C comparison with byte-identical injection between C conditions, not an in-car playback result.

An exploratory privileged target check on the same 12 DEV excerpts compared the **true four-role power mask** with its square root and the ideal bounded magnitude ratio mask. Median eligible/vocal transmission was **.660/.036** for power, **.770/.094** for square-root power and **.771/.105** for the ideal ratio mask. The trained role model's **.351/.110** lies far below the power target's retention and above its vocal transmission. The role-power mask is therefore not inherently too weak in this source-attribution diagnostic; it is a noncausal oracle, not a model result. [`diag_role_calibration.py`](RETIRED_TOOLS.md#tool-edd71d4c3c18) shows the confidence gap: on audible cells with true eligible power fraction ≥.8, the target mean eligible fraction was **.920** and the model predicted **.495**; **1.6%** of all time/frequency cells were audible and received model eligible probability >.8, versus **6.1%** audible cells with target eligibility >.8. On confidently vocal cells, it predicted eligible **.200** against a target **.028**. This is an underconfident ownership estimate, not merely a wrong global gain.

A further host-C oracle screen of the true role-power target on the same enriched DEV20 **passed all measured mechanism gates**: any-stem swings **7→0/20**, guitar/keys/piano/strings **.209/.227/.221/.223**, voice/bass/drums **.001/.004/.004**. Inputs were byte-identical within the C pair. This uses true stems unavailable to a live estimator, so the evaluator's generic “promote” label means only that the privileged representation met the screen.

The unchanged role-power oracle then passed **all measured full DEV40 C gates**: **8→0/40** any-stem swing songs, guitar/keys/piano/strings **.2086/.2157/.2204/.2144**, voice/bass/drums **.00103/.00449/.00294**, guitar/keys/wind level slopes **+.0221/+.0266/+.0200**, and rear RMS **+.3127 dB** against its own fresh control. Both conditions had 231 stem rows and 40 byte-identical injected WAV pairs. The full40 verdict is for a **privileged representation bound** only; it is DEV-selected, noncausal and unavailable on live stereo. `screen_role_power_oracle40/decision.json` on the PC records all gates. It confirms that the trained student's failure is not an inherent inability of the four-role power target plus this C route to meet the measured DEV40 limits.

An exploratory frozen-checkpoint temperature curve on the same 12 inspected DEV excerpts tested `softmax(logits / T)` without retraining. At **T=1/.75/.5/.33**, eligible transmission was **.351/.438/.538/.604** and vocal/eligible transmission ratio **.435/.415/.403/.400**; the anchor ratio was **.405**. Sharpening recovers absolute instruments but does not improve vocal selectivity meaningfully, let alone the preselected 20% target. Drums/eligible and bass/eligible improve modestly at T=.5, but the vocal ratio remains at the original frontier; no extra C sweep or product promotion follows from this post hoc calibration.

The trained role model fails the selection rule; no 10k continuation or full40 **student** promotion follows. The next one-change offline test should address confidence on the rare eligible-dominant cells directly, with class-balanced or contrastive supervision and the same gain-invariant source diagnostic before any C screen. That test must distinguish the source discriminator from gain adjustment; it cannot replace a new independent real-stem set, device timing/int8 measurement, Track A integration or the owner's deferred listening verdict.

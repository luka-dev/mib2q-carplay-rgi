# UPMIX rework proposal (2026-10-07)

*The redesign direction for a strong, stable rear scene with the front kept, produced on 2026-10-07 from the [rework baseline](UPMIX_REWORK_BASELINE_2026-10-07.md) by a panel of five independent proposals, three judges and an adversarial skeptic. Nothing here has been built, deployed or listened to; it is the plan to discuss with the owner before the first car session.*

---

## 0. Coordinator's notes (read first)

**How this was made.** Five designers wrote competing proposals from fixed angles (structural rear without semantics; objects done right on a static bed; two-layer scene with a safety boundary; cabin-first bus control; minimal justified learning). Three judges scored them from different lenses (the owner's ear, the embedded engineer, the evidence skeptic); a fourth agent tried to refute the leading thesis. Rankings: STRUCT-REAR first for two judges, the zero-training twin first for the engineer; DOSB (objects on a bed) last for all three. The plan below merges the two winners and grafts the useful parts of the others.

**The skeptic's verdict on the leading thesis ("the rear is faint because the architecture is subtractive and semantic-gated; a structural, static rear would be strong and stable") was `refuted` on four specifics, and those four are now in the plan as code changes, not prose:**

1. A louder Ls trips the cabin guard's linked six-channel scale (early-out only below 0.279 FS) and would pump the *front* on every peak with no release. Remedy: rear-first guard (§2.1 step 8) plus `guard_hits` counters.
2. A delayed coherent copy at near-equal level reads as width first and as "behind" only partly; the 17.09 "wow" ran with *full* transfer (retention 0: energy moved), not duplication. Remedy: alpha relocation of the coherent off-centre term is a staged lever (§2.4) and the bleed cancel is gated by a null test (§2.5).
3. A coherence-based ambient term can breathe with drum tails (the PAD baseline swung drums 18/40). Remedy: A capped 0.7, 88 ms symmetric EMA, 3-bin box, no front subtraction, and a harness regression gate before the car (§3).
4. Wide/doubled vocals and vocal reverb will be behind by construction. Remedy: the phase-2 whole-voice-gated duck, and owner question 1.

What the skeptic conceded survives unchanged: the faintness-by-construction diagnosis, the flicker root cause, "static content does not jump", the pan-projection swing mechanism, the centre-cancellation geometry for dry centred sources, the demotion of the nets to a floored protection duck, and the one-tone bleed test as the first cabin measurement the project would ever have.

**Two corrections to the plan body by the coordinator:**

- Session 0(a): `AUDIO_HSSR_MASK_DRIVE=0` does **not** switch the object layer off; it returns the object to the heuristic slot path (the 22.09-style objects, now with attack off and gate 0.5). Condition B therefore plays *heuristic objects + the capped beds + air −3 dB* with a bit-exact front at surround 0.5. It is still the right zero-code test (front untouched, rear at the parser maximum, with today's extraction), but a "voice top behind" finding there belongs partly to the heuristic objects, not only to the air copy. Read it as "does a louder, fuller rear with a bit-exact front read as behind or as wider, and does the guard pump", nothing more.
- Session 1 should include a third condition **B+α** (`FRONT_RETENTION` as alpha 0.35 on the coherent off-centre term only) rather than deferring relocation to session 3: the only in-car "wow" on record came with energy moved out of the front, and the `source_fft` path that makes a delay-aligned subtraction possible already exists. If B reads as "wider" and B+α as "behind", that single replug settles the precedence question the skeptic raised.

**Decision requested from the owner:** the eight questions in §6, above all question 8 (a stable wide stage with body first, or discrete instruments standing behind, which needs the runner-up and accepts phrase-rate movement) and question 1 (wide/doubled vocals audibly behind as "width": acceptable or not).

## 1. Diagnosis in five sentences

The rear is faint because, by construction, only what three nets confidently admit as "other" survives a stack of multiplicative vetoes ((1−0.92v)⁴·(1−0.85b)⁵·guard²·vocal gate), which on a centred pop mix is 3–18 % of the energy [offline 3.3b], and that remainder is then high-passed at 220 Hz, body-cut 0.05–0.85 up to 1 kHz plus −4 dB @ 500 Hz, capped at surround 0.40 (parser max 0.5) and half-duplicated in the front [code 1.5–1.9]. On the amp the result sits ~7 dB *under* the J525's own L→busLs −3 dB bleed (Ls −34 dBFS [offline] vs L at +6 dB on the same bus [model]), so the rear doors mostly play the front mix and the hook's contribution is a quieter, late, gated subset of the same thing: "mostly in the front" is arithmetic, not taste. The rear is unstable because every decision passes through fast-attack/slow-release memories (bin mask 0.50/0.020, band_mask 0.35/0.035, slot weight 0.34/0.010) driven by syllable-rate classifier outputs, and the rear amount depends on pan alignment through a rank-one projection (+6.14 dB per source even with true audio, 8/40 swings [oracle 28.09]) — proven root causes [offline 25.09], and every "stability fix" since 22.09 was one more veto, so each one thinned the rear further. The front is not kept either: the retention-0.5 transfer depletes the front mid 500–2000 Hz by −4.48 dB [model 2.11]. The root mechanism is therefore architectural: the rear is *subtractive and semantic-gated* where it should be *structural and static*; the only component that ever added rear without jumps was the static air copy [offline 25.09], and the passive side matrix sat on the student frontier for free (guitar .13 / keys .21 / voice .05 / bass .02 / drums .05, 4/39 swings [offline 3.3a]) and was never listened to.

## 2. The recommended architecture: STRUCT-REAR with grafts

Winner: STRUCT-REAR (ranked first by two of three judges, second by the embedded engineer who preferred its twin; the two are merged here). Grafts: single env switch, PAN_MAX cap, per-bin unity clamp, whole-voice-gated duck, q6d kept loaded for LFE (from Zero-training); self-stepping tone probe with offset sweep and same-sign S below 550 Hz (from Cabin-first); surround-0 A/B method, realtime middle tier, guard counter (from SPACE+PLACE); REAR_FLOOR off as a second A/B and the rear-RMS gate demoted (from DOSB). Three skeptic objections are answered with code changes, not prose: the cabin guard gets a rear-first remedy; surround starts at 0.50 not 0.55; the bleed cancel is gated by a probe that sweeps both delay readings.

### 2.1 Runtime signal chain (per hop, 2.667 ms; all in the existing STFT loop)

0. **Input and front** — unchanged: STFT 512/128 sine, 257 bins, bit-exact S16 front delay line 512 frames (`front_delay[2][512]`). Front L/R = delayed input × 0.958 with the −3 dB @ 200 Hz shelf. Transfer path fed zero (retention 1.0 is already bit-exact in `stereo_upmix.c:905-926`: multiplier 81920·(32768−q15)/32768 → 0, no code change).
1. **Per-bin statistics** — existing `scene_pl/scene_pr/scene_cross` EMA 0.06/hop (~45 ms, `hssr_dsp.c:1683-1694`) → `coh` as today; energy pan `e = (scene_pl − scene_pr)/(scene_pl + scene_pr)` from the same EMAs (the separate `scene_lr` 0.05 EMA is retired: one time constant).
2. **Structural gain** (new, ~40 flops/bin): `O = min(PAN_MAX, smoothstep(|e|, 0.24, 0.62)) · smoothstep(coh, 0.35, 0.60)` (q5's amplitude thresholds .12/.35 converted to energy pan; PAN_MAX default 0.7 = hard-panned source stays ≥ 3 dB under its own front level pre-amp); `A = 0.7 · (1 − smoothstep(coh, 0.25, 0.60))`; `g_raw = min(1, O + A) · hp`, hp = 0 for bins ≤ 94 Hz, 0.5 at 187 Hz, 1.0 from 281 Hz (rear HP ~190 Hz; body 250 Hz–1 kHz fully kept, where the amp's Ls door path peaks +4.5 dB @ 500 Hz [model]).
3. **Smoothing** — `g += 0.03·(g_raw − g)` per hop (symmetric, τ ≈ 88 ms, 10–90 % step ≈ 150 ms), then a 3-bin box in frequency. No attack/release asymmetry, no hold, no slot, no threshold, no transient detector.
4. **Static floor** — `F = 0` below 190 Hz; 0.16 (−16 dB, `AUDIO_STEREO_REAR_FLOOR_DB`) 190 Hz–2 kHz; smoothstep 2–4 kHz up to the existing `band_air` 0.355 (−9 dB, `AUDIO_STEREO_REAR_AIR_DB`). `R = max(G, F)`: the rear never empties; mono masters still get a quiet second scene.
5. **Bed write** (replaces the object/steer/projection block at `hssr_dsp.c:1868-1940`): `bed_L = R·L + s·S`, `bed_R = R·R ∓ s·S`, `S = 0.5(L−R)`, `s = 0.25`; anti-phase (−) in bands with lower edge > 550 Hz, **same sign (+) at and below 550 Hz** so the pair cannot carve a low-mid hole at the rear seats, and S cancels in the amp's Ls+Rs → LFE sum above 550 Hz. Per-bin unity clamp: if `|bed_ch| > max(|L|,|R|)` scale the pair down. Object arrays `fft_re/im` = 0; `source_fft` = 0 and the second (source-image) iFFT skipped unless alpha > 0.
6. **Decorrelation** — `spa_hssr_mix_bed` unchanged (`0.79·now + 0.60·delayed_same − 0.14·delayed_cross`, Ls 32/24/10.7/5.3/2.7 ms, Rs 40/18.7/16/8/5.3 ms per band group; Ls/Rs MSC 2–8 kHz 0.50 → 0.40 [offline]). One packed Hermitian iFFT + WOLA → rear float L/R.
7. **Time domain** (`stereo_upmix.c` post chain): rear body shelf default 0 dB (knob −6..0; −2 is the ready escape for "bass everywhere"); LFE V2 unchanged; +3 dB @ 4 kHz rear shelf kept; linked rear limiter kept, release 0.0005 → 0.00014/sample (~150 ms) so it cannot breathe at transient rate; rear-only delay 576 frames = 12 ms kept (becomes `AUDIO_STEREO_REAR_DELAY_MS`, 4..12, ring already 576); `rear_out = clamp(rear) × SURROUND`, **default 0.50**, parser cap `SPA_STEREO_UPMIX_REAR_Q15_MAX` 16384 → 23170 (0.71).
8. **Cabin guard, rear-first** (`spa_upmix_cabin_guard_s16`, lines 410–492): keep the LFE-first bound and the 0.985 FS limit; before the linked six-channel scale, scale **Ls/Rs only** by the factor that brings the worst bus under the limit, then fall through to the linked scale only if a bus is still over. Every Ls-fed bus has a front term ≤ 0.708·|L| < 0.985, so rear-first always resolves an Ls-driven overload and the front is never touched by the guard. Add `guard_hits` and `guard_linked` per-block counters to the health line.
9. **Switch** — `AUDIO_HSSR_STRUCTURAL=1` selects all of the above inside the same `.so`; 0 is bit-identical Track A (object path, mask, vetoes untouched). In structural mode: q6d stays loaded and running (42 M MAC/s) so LFE V2's bass/drum confidences are unchanged (no silent sub-level change: the 19.09 rule) and its `p_vocal` is available for the phase-2 duck; V11 and the mask net are not loaded; the 3-late-controls failsafe decay (0.75) and `mask_fallbacks` do not touch the structural gains; "model missing → upmix off" is unchanged because q6d is still required.
10. **Realtime middle tier** — on an overrun strike, keep the structural rear (it costs about the no-net DSP) rather than silencing the rears; the 4-strike full disable stays as last resort.

### 2.2 What each channel receives

- **L/R**: bit-exact input, 10.667 ms late, × 0.958, −3 dB @ 200 Hz. Identical to today minus the transfer (≈ +3 dB broadband, mids back).
- **C**: 0. **LFE**: 0.125 FS LR4-60 mono of the front, unchanged.
- **Ls/Rs**: 0.50 × [12 ms late, limited, +3 dB @ 4 kHz] of the comb-decorrelated sum of: off-centre coherent content at its own L/R image (≤ 0.7), ambient residual (≤ 0.7 × (1−coh)), side term ±0.25·S, and the static floor (−16 dB mids / −9 dB top); all ≥ 190 Hz, nothing from a classifier.

Level arithmetic [model, kInputMatrix 0.708/1.995/2.818]: a hard-panned guitar g reaches the left door bus at 0.68g (bleed) + 1.995·0.5·0.7g = 0.70g → 1.38g vs 0.79g on the front bus: the rear copy is electrically above the front copy for that guitar, ~17 ms late (corrected 2026-10-17 from 24 ms). A dry centred lead reaches the doors at 0.708L + 1.995·0.5·0.16L = 0.87L: +1.8 dB over the stock bleed, static. Rear RMS lands around −12..−15 dBFS on typical mixes versus −34 dBFS today.

### 2.3 Protection

By construction, zero time constant: a dry centred lead, main bass, kick and snare have |e| ≈ 0 and coh ≈ 1 in their own bins (lead vocal and bass 100 % centre in MoisesDB [offline 3.3b]), so O = 0, A = 0, S = 0 sample-exactly; they reach the rear only through the static floor (−16 dB mids, −9 dB top), ~17 ms late (corrected 2026-10-17 from 24 ms). A vocal entry into a bin shared with a panned guitar moves that bin's pan toward centre with τ 45 ms and the gain follows with τ 88 ms: the guitar's shared bins dip smoothly by a few dB, nothing else moves, nothing holds, nothing releases slower than it attacks.

Phase 2 only, if the owner hears a wide/doubled vocal behind (knob `AUDIO_HSSR_VOCAL_DUCK_DB` off / −6 / −12, default off): per band 280 Hz–8.2 kHz, `D = max(floor, 1 − 0.75·smoothstep(p_vocal_q6d, 0.5, 0.9)·vocal_presence)` applied to O, A and S only (never to F or air); attack = the control it arrives on (10.7 ms), release EMA 0.04/control (~270 ms); gated by the existing global `vocal_presence` (EMA 0.10/0.02) so a single-band false call cannot pull one guitar band forward; V11 not consumed (its "everything sustained is vocal" bias is documented [synth/offline]). Mask net retired.

### 2.4 Front policy

Bit-exact by default. Optional static partial relocation (session 3, `AUDIO_STEREO_FRONT_RETENTION` reinterpreted as alpha 0..0.5): `front −= alpha · O · [L, R]` on the coherent off-centre component only, delay-aligned through the existing `source_fft` path, never the ambient/side/floor copies (owner 10-03: β ≥ α, never subtract a decorrelated copy). This is the depth lever if the rear reads as "wider" rather than "behind".

### 2.5 Amp interaction

- Bleed: addressed first by level (2.2), second by the optional cancel `AUDIO_STEREO_BLEED_CANCEL=k:offset` (default `0:0`, OFF). `Ls −= k · shelf(0.958 · x_L(t − offset))` from the input ring, Rs mirrored. k = 0.251 nulls the +9 dB effect bus (0.708 − 2.818·0.251 = 0.0007) and leaves −13.7 dB residual on the doors; k = 0.355 nulls the doors but inverts the pillars to −10.7 dB, so 0.25 is the operating point. **The offset is unknown**: the baseline (1.11) lists 403 vs 87 samples as *bus* delays after the matrix (same-frame cancel, offset 0); STRUCT-REAR and SPACE+PLACE read them as input-lane delays (offset 316). The probe in §5 sweeps both; a wrong offset is a −9 dB comb into the rear doors, so the knob ships off. Enabling it also removes the amp's bass bleed from the doors (against the 19.09 wish) — the owner decides after hearing it.
- Rear drivers: doors HP ~100 Hz, notches 113/138/174 Hz, Ls path +4.5 dB @ 500 Hz [model]; hence HP ~190 Hz, body kept, shelf default 0. C-pillar effect speakers at +9 dB carry the air; the +3 dB @ 4 kHz shelf stays.
- Headroom: hook rear ≤ 0.5 FS; effect bus ≤ 0.678·|L| + 1.41·|rear_pre| binds at coincident full-scale panned peaks above ~0.47 FS; the rear-first guard clips the rear there, not the front. The amp's own door limiter group 0.81..0.95 (feedback, no look-ahead) is the next ceiling; `SURROUND 0.45` is the lever if rear squash is heard on loud masters.
- Delay: 12 ms hook + ~5.2 ms amp rear-door lag = ~17 ms behind front mid/treble (corrected 2026-10-17 from 12 + 6.6 bus + 5.2 output = ~24 ms: the 5.2 ms total already contains the bus delay): inside the echo threshold and the precedence window. Honest statement: a delayed coherent copy at near-equal level reads as width/size first and as "behind" only partly; depth beyond that comes from alpha or the bleed cancel, both staged.
- First-B spatial upmixer state unknown; MMI Surround untouched during tests.

### 2.6 Compute and latency

Audio thread: the structural block replaces the slot search, ridge gate, per-bin comodulation (one software `logf` per bin per hop), universal/diffuse targets, projection/steering and three per-bin EMAs; cost at or below the measured music-stage no-net 1.81 ms per 21.3 ms block [log 1.13] versus 4.28 ms mean today; one iFFT per hop instead of two. Worker: q6d only, 42 M MAC/s (was 84 M + mask 1.33 ms/control), under the 150 M gate by 3.5×. No new buffers (bed arrays, ring, input ring exist); −0.6 MB mask working set. Latency unchanged: 10.667 ms front, +12 ms rear; `ALL_OUT_LATENCY 80` untouched. No allocation, locks or logging on the audio thread.

## 3. Why it will be stable and strong, and what the owner will hear

- **No asymmetric memories** [offline 25.09 root cause]: the only state is two symmetric EMAs (45 ms statistics, 88 ms gain). The skeptic is right that first-order smoothing attenuates 4–8 Hz modulation by only ~7–13 dB; what removes the "goes back and front" event is not the smoothing alone but three things together: nothing can *flip* (gains are Lipschitz functions of bounded ratios), nothing *holds* (no slot/owner/latch can send the next syllable to the rear), and the front *does not move* (the perceived jump needs the front to drop as the rear rises; today's transfer and the owner attack did exactly that).
- **No pan projection** [oracle 28.09: 8/40 swings with true audio from the projection; direct own-image route 0/40]: rear amount = level × gain, level-invariant ratios, so depth cannot follow loudness.
- **Static layers dominate the rear energy**: the floor and air copy are mix copies at fixed gain (the one component measured not to jump, flick250 +0.014 [offline 25.09]); the side term is a fixed linear matrix (4/39 guitar swings [offline 3.3a]); only O and A move, both smoothed, A capped 0.7. The PAD baseline's drums 18/40 [offline] is the named hazard: it had an uncapped a = 1−coh at 50 ms *with front subtraction* and a 300 Hz floor; here A is capped, 88 ms-smoothed, 3-bin-boxed and the front is untouched. The pre-car harness gate: structural rear must swing no more than the passive-matrix row at ≥ 2× its guitar/keys share; if it fails, gain EMA 150 ms and A cap 0.5 before the car, never a transient detector.
- **Rear never empties**: R ≥ F by construction; the vocal-entry duck that is structural to any extracted rear (20 % median / 58 % p10 [offline 2.11]) becomes a bounded dip of shared bins only.
- **Remaining fast gain paths, named**: rear limiter (150 ms release, rarely engaged below 1.0 with the unity clamp), rear-first guard (rear only, counted), amp door limiter (uncontrollable, lever = surround). Cold start: ~100–150 ms versus 0.3–1 s today.

What the owner will hear: a continuous wide scene behind on every stereo track — panned guitars/keys at their own left/right behind him with body, the room and reverb behind, hats and air as today — that does not jump per hit or syllable; the lead, bass, kick and snare in front; a front that is the stock front again (≈ +3 dB, mids back, so the A/B must be level-matched). Compared with 22.09 ("vivid, they flew"): fewer bright objects flying, more a stable stage with body. What will be missing versus "instruments behind you": a dry centred instrument (centred piano, centred synth lead) never goes behind; bass guitar is behind only as −16 dB harmonics 250 Hz–1 kHz (its fundamental cannot reach the doors, HP 100 Hz; Ls→LFE −14 dB [model]); on narrow/mono masters the rear is the floor plus whatever reverb exists (thin, honestly); wide/doubled vocals and vocal reverb *will* be behind at ambient level (the 09-13 policy relaxed; the duck is the mitigation); the first reaction at 0.50 may be "too much" before the knob goes up.

## 5. The listening-first plan

Rules: one lever per replug (env is read per dio_manager generation); the agent never reboots the unit; phone mic at the driver's headrest for every pair; verdicts written per song before the next song; the frozen offline gates are not release criteria (they pin rear RMS at −37 dBFS ±0.5 dB and will fail every design by construction [offline 2.11]).

**Session 0 — day 1, zero hook code, ~1.5 h in the car.**
(a) Skeptic's env-only premise test on the shipped build: B = `MASK_DRIVE=0, FRONT_RETENTION=1.0, REAR_BODY_DB=0, REAR_AIR_DB=−3, SURROUND=0.5`; A = same with `SURROUND=0`. Front bit-identical, volume untouched. Three 60 s sections (doubled-vocal pop, hard-panned rock, dense loud master). Questions: stronger rear scene, and "behind" or "wider/louder"? lead or its double behind? anything moving per hit/syllable? front dips on peaks (mic: broadband dips coincident with rear peaks = guard)? Reading: this plays today's capped beds (ambience ≤ 0.04, universal ≤ 0.55·AGC) plus the air copy at −3 dB, so a *faint* verdict is not a refutation; front dips on peaks and "voice top behind" are real findings and set the guard remedy and the air default before any code is written.
(b) Tone probe, ~25 lines in the post chain, `AUDIO_STEREO_MATRIX_PROBE=1`: outputs L = input L, R = C = LFE = Rs = 0, Ls = −k·L(t − offset), k stepping {0, .20, .25, .30, .355, .40, .50} every 8 s with a 100 ms 2 kHz blip, two runs with offset {0, 316} samples (plus ±8 around the better one). Source: local WAV from the phone (Files/VLC, not Spotify), left-only tone at −20 dBFS, 250/500/1000/3000 Hz. Listener or mic at the rear-left door woofer, then the left C-pillar. Pass: door near-silent at one k (expected ≈ 0.355) and the pillar at a lower k (≈ 0.25) at one offset, same at all four frequencies. Rising level with k = inverted polarity (flip sign). No null at either offset = the [model] matrix or an active first-B upmixer; the cancel stays off for good and the design loses only that lever. This is the first cabin measurement the project has ever had; it does not block session 1 (the probe build and the structural build are one zip).

**Session 1 — day 3–4, first structural listen.** One `.so`; conditions by replug: A = `AUDIO_HSSR_STRUCTURAL=0` (bit-identical Track A); B = structural, `SURROUND 0.50, REAR_FLOOR_DB −16, REAR_AIR_DB −9, REAR_BODY_DB 0, PAN_MAX 0.7, retention 1.0, BED_DELAYS on, BLEED_CANCEL off, VOCAL_DUCK off`; B0 = B with `SURROUND 0`. Order per song: B0 → B → A → B. B0/B is level-matched by construction (same front PCM); for A/B match A-weighted RMS within 0.5 dB by MMI volume (B's front is ~3 dB louder than A's by design — tell the owner beforehand that the front is now the stock front). Five songs, 24–60 s: (1) centred dry-lead pop, (2) rock with hard-panned guitars and real room, (3) wide/doubled-vocal pop, (4) narrow/mono-ish master, (5) dense loud modern master. Questions in order: is there a clearly stronger rear scene than B0, and does it read as behind or as wider? is the dry lead behind (song 1)? is the double/reverb behind and does it bother you (song 3)? does anything move per hit or syllable (any yes is a bug, not a tuning)? is the front identical to B0 (it must be)? does the rear squash or the front dip on peaks (song 5; read `guard_hits` after)? Rules: song 2 "clearly behind and stable" + song 1 "no lead behind" → advance. Song 1 lead behind → next replug `REAR_FLOOR_DB off` (then −20). Movement heard → gain EMA 150 ms, A cap 0.5, re-screen on the harness before the next replug. "Still faint" → `SURROUND 0.60`. Song 3 "voice behind, bothers me" → session 2 duck. Song 5 pumping → `SURROUND 0.45`, read the counters.

**Session 2 — one lever per replug, same method (B0 vs candidate).** In order of likelihood: `REAR_FLOOR_DB=off` (DOSB's "bed-only" analogue: is the −16 dB mix copy heard as a copy, or as the thing that keeps the rear alive?); `SURROUND 0.60`; `REAR_BODY_DB −2` if 350–800 Hz reads as "bass everywhere" (17.09 [car]); `VOCAL_DUCK_DB −12` then −6 if wide vocals are behind; `BED_DELAYS off` if sustained panned parts sound phasey; `REAR_DELAY_MS 8`.

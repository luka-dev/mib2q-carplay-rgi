## Question and architecture

The existing causal matrix student uses recurrent features yet fails protected-source and vocal-entry gates. Test whether an **explicit, learnable eligible spatial state from mixture alone** helps, while keeping the current matrix output and a fast independent protection cue.

Use the review-29 causal 2×2 matrix trunk and source loss, with 36/24/24/24 channels, zero lookahead, 2 s causal running-level normalization, seed 1 and the same truth/pseudo training pools. At each frame and band, a current-frame head predicts vocal/drum/bass/eligible role logits. A separate projection of the causal band feature proposes eligible spatial evidence. Its contribution is weighted by the role head's eligible probability and accumulated with an **8 s exponential state**. The decoder sees the slow state and the fast role logits alongside its original causal band/global/frame features. The role target is supervised by the existing four-source power cross-entropy. True stems are **training targets only**; inference sees stereo mixture alone. The added projections and decoder inputs must keep the estimated neural load below 150 M MAC/s before FFT, state arithmetic and int8 implementation; only device measurement can establish product fit.

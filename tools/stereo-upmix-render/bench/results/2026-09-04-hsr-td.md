# `hsr-td` implementation, tuning, and evaluation

Recorded on 2026-09-04. The measured `hook/audio/stereo_upmix.c` SHA-256 is
`3314cec5210d0958146521db21231c33c06be58bbe64685e3c0386c98d5df6df`.

`hsr-td` is a clean-room causal time-domain spatial-field decoder. It uses the
shared complementary six-band IIR bank, smooths a real 2x2 covariance per band,
refreshes its principal projector every eight samples, retains the positive
coherent component in the dry fronts, and sends the unexplained residual to
Ls/Rs. It has no FFT, heap work, lookahead, block-fill delay, or artificial
decorrelator. It is based only on the public high-level HSR idea and does not
claim DAM Audio source, coefficient, or output parity.

## Covariance tuning and safety probes

Only the covariance time constants changed. The correlation knee remains
`0.45..0.92`: wider alternatives improved some fixture metrics but leaked
near-coherent center material. The selected compile-time defaults are attack
`0.020` (about 1 ms) and release `0.0026` (about 8 ms), replacing `0.040` and
`0.0013`. They are not runtime/environment controls.

| corpus | setting | rear env corr | rear waveform corr | rear SI-SDR dB | rear L/R corr error | rear energy ratio | fold-back SNR dB |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 9 development songs | old | 0.522 | 0.251 | -12.09 | 1.779 | 0.029 | 41.26 |
| 9 development songs | selected | 0.518 | 0.264 | -11.65 | 1.743 | 0.030 | 41.26 |
| 24 held-out songs | old | 0.437 | 0.371 | -8.93 | 1.236 | 0.045 | 34.40 |

| authored Sintel excerpt | selected | 0.837 | 0.221 | -12.90 | 0.678 | 0.157 | 33.28 |

This is a measured tradeoff: waveform correlation, SI-SDR, and rear image
error improve, while envelope correlation and fold-back SNR regress slightly
on held-out material. A faster release improved the first group further but
was rejected to limit energy pumping.

## Multigenre and authored-5.1 fold-down evaluation

The reproducible corpus uses the official
[MUSDB18](https://sigsep.github.io/datasets/musdb.html) sample and the official
[MedleyDB sample](https://medleydb.weebly.com/downloads.html). Nine development
fixtures cover 80s Rock, Beatles-style, Britpop, Disco, Country, Gospel,
Grunge, Singer/Songwriter, and World/Folk. Another 24 deterministically selected
MUSDB tracks are held out from tuning. Source audio stays under ignored
`build/reference-audio/` and is not redistributed.

These are deterministic spatial fixtures derived from multitrack stems, not
artist-authored 5.1 masters. A fold-down is non-invertible, so the figures are
comparative/regression evidence and cannot establish listening quality.
Waveform metrics also penalize intentional decorrelation. Center is excluded
because the hook emits digital zero for the vehicle SoundAmp to derive. The
following rear metrics use LFE off and rear gain 0.18.

## LFE bass-assist evaluation

All three rear backends share the exact same LFE path:

```text
LFE = (5833 / 32768) * LR4_80Hz((L + R) / 2)
front L/R gain = 30802 / 32768
```

LFE is omitted from the stereo fold, so this cannot recover the original
discrete LFE channel. It measures whether the restrained bass assist follows
the reference low-frequency envelope. The selected Sintel excerpt has a
digital-zero reference LFE, so its target metrics are correctly reported as
`n/a` rather than as a meaningless ratio.

| corpus | LFE env corr | LFE SI-SDR dB | LFE energy ratio | SoundAmp bus peak mean/max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 9 development songs | 0.816 | 4.22 | 1.232 | 0.441 / 0.620 |
| 24 held-out songs | 0.603 | 1.13 | 0.769 | 0.425 / 0.503 |

The visible recovered SoundAmp buses remain below full scale in every corpus
render. The front/fold-back SNR with assist is about 24 dB because the profile
deliberately trims fronts to 0.940 for matrix headroom; it is not HSR-TD
distortion. Physical sub polarity, crossover, and acoustic summation still
require the stationary vehicle test.

Per-track results and WAVs are generated locally in
`build/reference-audio/hsr-td-heldout-eval-{off,assist}/`.

## Regression gates

The host audio suite passes for HSR-TD, including exact mono, fixed and moving
positive-pan rejection, diffuse-to-dialogue cleanup, near-coherent center
rejection, anti-phase passband survival, exact channel-swap equivariance,
immediate causal output, arbitrary chunk invariance, reset/tail removal,
saturation, overlap safety, LFE LR4 response/polarity/anti-phase null, recovered
SoundAmp row bounds, and AAC/APAC codec-switch resets. ASan/UBSan and the
complete GCC 8.5 hook build also pass.

The remaining gates are a level-matched stationary vehicle A/B and real
on-unit callback p99 timing. Startup therefore remains `ambient-lite`.
